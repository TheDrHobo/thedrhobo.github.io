# Welcome to Dr Hobo's Blog

**Dr Hobo** likes reading, playing video games, and has been known to watch films. He also travels and enjoys playing cards or a new board game. This is a corner of the internet to commemorate these interests in the form of top ten lists. Comment on these musings if you'd like to, or don't.
