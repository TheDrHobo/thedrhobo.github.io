## Itacaré, Bahia, Brazil

**Last visit:** *[to be added]*  
**Vibe:** Jungle meets surf town — raw, beautiful, unhurried

*[Placeholder — full post coming soon.]*

Tucked into the lush Atlantic Forest coastline of Bahia, Itacaré is the kind of place that makes you cancel your onward ticket. A string of near-deserted beaches accessible only by jungle trail, a main street of caipirinha bars and barefoot surfers, and the constant soundtrack of howler monkeys overhead — it is, quite simply, one of the most quietly extraordinary places I've ever landed.

The beaches here don't compete for your attention; they simply reveal themselves, one after another, each more improbable than the last. Engenhoca, Havaizinho, Prainha — each requires a short trek through Atlantic rainforest, which only adds to the sense of discovery.
